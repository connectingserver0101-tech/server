About us

Need a place where you can work or study in peace? You’ve come to the right spot.

At Quiet Corner you’ll find silence, a cozy atmosphere, and hot drinks that warm you up in any weather. Take the table by the window and stay as long as you like.

  • Quiet by designSoft music, no loud calls.
  • Made for long sessionsFree Wi-Fi and a power outlet at every table.
  • Student friendlyRefills of brewed coffee for 1.50 ₼.
